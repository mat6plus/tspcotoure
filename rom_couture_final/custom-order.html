Phase 03

Fit & Measurements

Provide measurements in CM if known. We will review dimensions with you during consultation.

How to measure precisely
  • Chest: around the fullest part, tape horizontal.
  • Waist: around your natural waistline.
  • Shoulder: from one shoulder bone edge to the other.
  • Sleeve: shoulder joint to wrist joint.
  • Neck: where a collar rests comfortably.
  • Length: back neck joint to desired hem height.
