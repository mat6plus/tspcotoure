The Origin

Rooted in West African Textile Heritage

Our founder's earliest memories are of a grandmother's loom — the rhythmic beat of a shuttle weaving Kente strips into ceremonial cloth that would one day mark milestones of a family's life.

From our New York atelier to the world, TSP Couture dresses professionals, brides, and cultural ambassadors who demand something the high street cannot offer: a garment that fits them, not the mannequin.

“Every thread carries intention. We do not make clothes. We make heirlooms.”

— Founder, TSP Couture

Why we do not accept returns

Each TSP Couture garment is constructed from scratch, entirely for you. From the moment we source your chosen fabric to the final quality-control press, every resource is committed to a single person's vision and measurements.

Because no two pieces are alike, we invest heavily at the front end: detailed intake, measurement guidance, fabric confirmation, and direct consultation.
